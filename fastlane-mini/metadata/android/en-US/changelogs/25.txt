MasterClock Light has been renamed MasterClock Lite. If you use that build, the rename changed its identity and Android will no longer offer it updates: install MasterClock Lite from the release page and remove the old one. This build is unaffected and updates normally.
This build's icon no longer carries a FULL badge.
Releases now ship a SHA256SUMS.txt so you can check the APK you downloaded, and release tags are signed from this version on.
Restoring a backup: an oversized archive is refused as it arrives, instead of being copied to the cache in full and rejected afterwards.
The Lato typeface used by the E-Ink build is credited in Credits.
