A voice note recorded for the full minute was always lost, and the microphone kept recording after the screen said it had stopped. The one-minute limit only ever changed the label; it now actually ends the recording.
Leaving a voice note while it was still recording threw the take away, because the recorder was released without being stopped. It is kept now.
Importing a JSON file said nothing at all, whether it worked or not: the picker closed and the screen was unchanged either way. Both outcomes are reported now.
The precision trainer measured with wall-clock time, which a sync can move mid-attempt, and kept the reading from its last poll instead of from your tap — on the one screen where the measurement is the whole exercise.
Retaking a video kept showing the previous one.
The clock no longer stops to serialise the notebook every fifteen seconds, and the game history no longer decodes on the thread drawing the clock — nor without any limit when history is set to unlimited.
Typing in a note no longer rewrites the entire notebook on every keystroke. Edits are saved after a short pause, and immediately if you leave the note.
Link board: a board dropping out could cost the phone a Bluetooth connection slot when the app had lost the permission, because closing was skipped while the reference to it was cleared anyway.
Two buttons and two error messages that were always in English are translated.
A debug build no longer replaces an installed release build. This only affects builds made from source.
